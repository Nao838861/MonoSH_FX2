=== death === both uninterruptible
set_instrument_and_adsr se_lead 15 3 3 20
set_volume 210
play_note c5 no_keyoff 4
portamento_calc c2 keyoff 35 40
set_instrument_and_adsr se_bass 15 5 1 22
play_noise 18 28

=== boss_explosion === both uninterruptible
set_instrument_and_adsr se_bass 15 5 2 22
set_volume 240
start_loop 4
play_noise 16 10
play_noise 12 12
end_loop

=== stumble === one uninterruptible
set_instrument_and_adsr se_bass 15 5 1 24
set_volume 180
play_note e3 no_keyoff 3
portamento_calc c2 keyoff 12 18

=== explosion === one interruptible
; 敵の撃破。割れる音から低い地鳴りへノイズを下げ、0.6秒ほど響かせる。
; 連続で倒すと鳴り直しで「ぼぼぼ」と刻まれ、最後の一つが「ぼーん」と残る。
set_instrument_and_adsr se_bass 15 2 0 16
set_volume 230
play_noise 26 no_keyoff 2
play_noise 18 no_keyoff 3
play_noise 14 no_keyoff 6
play_noise 12 no_keyoff 12
play_noise 10 no_keyoff 20
play_noise 9 30

=== ground_explosion === one interruptible
; 地形（木・茂み・岩）の破壊。敵より重く、崩れる音を挟んで長く響く。

set_volume 240
play_noise 28 no_keyoff 2
play_noise 21 no_keyoff 3
play_noise 15 no_keyoff 5
play_noise 17 no_keyoff 3
play_noise 13 no_keyoff 8
play_noise 15 no_keyoff 3
play_noise 11 no_keyoff 14
play_noise 10 no_keyoff 20
play_noise 8 35
